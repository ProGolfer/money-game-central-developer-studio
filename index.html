Money Game Central Design Philosophy

⭐ The North Star

Every design decision must improve the golfer's Saturday experience.

1. Today Comes First

The Dashboard focuses only on today's league experience. Season statistics, history and awards belong in their own areas.

2. One-Tap Experience

The most important Saturday actions should always be one tap away.

3. Progressive Reveal

Information unlocks naturally as Saturday moves through registration, teams, live play, submitted results and the official recap.

4. Golfer First

Every player should immediately understand where they are, what they can do and what happens next.

5. History Lives Forever

Every completed round becomes part of the player's permanent profile, scorecard archive and league history.

6. Simplicity Wins

Powerful features should never make the app feel crowded, confusing or difficult to use.

7. Build Once. Build Right.

Screens are perfected inside Developer Studio, approved, frozen and then moved into production.

North Star Check

Does it improve the golfer's Saturday experience?Required
Does it belong on this screen?Required
Does it make the app simpler?Required
Final Rule

When in doubt, follow The North Star.

This philosophy guides every screen, feature and design decision inside Money Game Central.
